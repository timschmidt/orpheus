Orpheus

A new firmware for the Yoton YM03 MP3 player, written from scratch in Rust. Gapless music and MIDI, a library, radio, books, games, a screen reader and Bluetooth audio on a 128 × 128 screen with 320 KiB of RAM.

Try it in your browser Install on your YM03

What it does

works has been used on the player. being checked runs on the player and passes its automated tests there, but a person has not yet checked all of it.

  • Playback works

    MP3, FLAC, WAV, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and Speex; gapless joins; resume after power-off or a restart; queue, shuffle, A–B repeat, bookmarks.

  • MIDI works

    MIDI and karaoke files played by Orpheus's own synthesiser and General MIDI sound set; the clock rises to 288 MHz when a song needs it.

  • Library works

    Artists, albums, genres and recently added, built in the background; folders sorted instantly from an on-card index.

  • Sound works

    Equaliser with presets and custom curves, bass, crossfeed, balance, ReplayGain, fade on pause.

  • FM radio works

    Tuner with presets, scanning and regions, using the hardware's analogue path; stereo and RDS are being checked.

  • Bluetooth audio works

    Pair and connect headphones or speakers; reconnects by itself.

  • Pictures works

    JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF and QOI, fit or fill, rotation, slideshow; album covers on Now Playing.

  • Games works

    Blocks, 4096, Solitaire, the artillery duel Salvo and the brick breaker Bricks, with settings, scores and saved games.

  • Books works

    TXT, Markdown, FB2 and EPUB with contents, bookmarks and reading settings. Reading aloud is planned.

  • Firmware updates works

    Written next to the running firmware and used only once complete; three failed starts fall back to the previous version.

  • USB card reader works

    The player's card appears on the computer; a developer channel in every USB mode.

  • USB disk and CD works

    An ISO or disk image on the card shown to a computer; starting a real PC from it is being checked.

  • Screen reader being checked

    Every screen read aloud by Orpheus's own English speech voice; larger text, high-contrast themes, slower keys.

  • Recorder being checked

    Microphone and radio recording to WAV, ADPCM or FLAC, with automatic gain and voice activation.

  • Steps, stopwatch, timer, alarm being checked

    Step counter from the accelerometer with a goal and a week's history; stopwatch with laps, timer, alarms.

  • Authenticator being checked

    One-time codes behind a PIN, typed into the computer as a USB keyboard, with honest limits.

  • Sequencer simulator only

    A drum machine with synth tracks, songs and export to WAV and MIDI; built and tested in the simulator, hidden from Home there; not on the player yet.

  • Presenter remote investigating

    Click through slides over Bluetooth, if the module can carry the key reports.

  • USB sound card planned

    The player as a computer's audio interface: music to the headphones and Bluetooth, the microphone to the computer.

  • Jack tools planned

    Signal generator, infrared remote and peripheral control through adapters in the headphone jack.
