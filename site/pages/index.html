Orpheus

A new firmware for the Yoton YM03 MP3 player, written from scratch in Rust. Gapless music, a library, pictures and Bluetooth audio on a 128 × 128 screen with 320 KiB of RAM.

Try it in your browser coming Install on your YM03

Screens

Real screens, rendered by the same code that runs on the player (the golden-image tests).

What it does

  • Playback works

    MP3, FLAC, WAV, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and Speex; gapless joins; resume after power-off; queue, shuffle, A–B repeat, bookmarks.

  • Library works

    Artists, albums, genres and recently added, built in the background; folders sorted instantly from an on-card index.

  • Sound works

    Equaliser with presets and custom curves, bass, crossfeed, balance, ReplayGain.

  • Pictures works

    JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF and QOI, fit or fill, rotation, slideshow; album covers on Now Playing.

  • Bluetooth audio works

    Pair and connect headphones or speakers; reconnects by itself.

  • USB card reader works

    The player's card appears on the computer; a developer channel in every USB mode.

  • USB disk and CD in progress

    Boot a computer from an ISO or disk image on the card.

  • Presenter remote investigating

    Click through slides over Bluetooth, if the module can carry the key reports.

  • FM radio planned

    Tuner with presets, using the hardware's analogue path.

  • Recorder planned

    Microphone and radio recording.

  • Authenticator planned

    One-time codes typed over USB, with honest limits.

  • Steps and stopwatch planned

    Pedometer from the accelerometer; stopwatch and timer.
