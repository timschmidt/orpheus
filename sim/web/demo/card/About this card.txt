Orpheus demo card

This card holds real works, one for every kind of file the player opens,
all of them in the public domain or under a free licence. CREDITS.txt
names every one: who made it, its licence, where it comes from and what
was changed (most are short excerpts).

  Beethoven, Symphony No. 5 (Skidmore College Orchestra): Ogg Vorbis
  Caruso, "Vesti la giubba" (1907) with its words: Opus and LRC lyrics
  Chopin, Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 (Peter Johnston): AIFF
  Satie, Gymnopedie No. 1 (Daria Baiocchi): WAV
  Sousa's Band, The Stars and Stripes Forever (1909 cylinder): Sun AU
  Bulfinch, The Age of Fable: Orpheus and Eurydice (LibriVox): Speex
  Demo tour.m3u8: a playlist of the music

MIDI

Pictures
  Corot, Orpheus Leading Eurydice (JPEG); the Ring Nebula in Lyra (PNG);
  Hokusai, The Great Wave (BMP); Earthrise (QOI); Muybridge, The Horse
  in Motion (GIF)

Books
  Bulfinch, The Age of Fable (EPUB) and Browning, The Pied Piper of
  Hamelin (plain text), both from Project Gutenberg

In the browser, files you drop onto the player go into "Your files". They
stay in your browser (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded.
