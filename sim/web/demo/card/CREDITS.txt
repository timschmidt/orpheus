Credits for the Orpheus demo card
=================================

Every picture, recording and book on this card is a real work by the
people named below, in the public domain or under a free licence.
None of it was generated. Where a file was trimmed, resized or
converted, that is said under "Changes".

Licences: PD = public domain; CC0 = https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/;
CC BY / BY-SA: the licence link is given with the work. Files made
from a CC BY-SA work are under the same licence.

This is the browser's card: short excerpts. The demo card for the
player (`cargo xtask demo-card`) adds the complete recordings and
full-size pictures.
